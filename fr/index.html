Getting started

From book to bandstand in three steps.

TuneFinder searches indexes of the songbooks you own. Connect a PDF to an index and a search result can take you directly to the corresponding lead-sheet page.

01

Prepare your library

Add a book index and its PDF

Open Books from the TuneFinder home screen. Find a book you own and tap Download index to make its tune list searchable.

  1. Choose a book and download its index.
  2. Tap Choose PDF… and select your copy of that book.
  3. Once your index and PDF are linked, return to the home screen.

TuneFinder does not provide the songbook PDF. You connect a legal copy already stored on your device.

02

Find a tune

Search by title and open the exact page

Start typing any part of a tune title. Results appear as you type, with the matching book and printed page number shown on each card.

  1. Enter a full or partial tune title in the search field.
  2. Choose the result from the book you want to use.
  3. TuneFinder opens the connected PDF at the matching page, so the lead sheet is ready to read.

Page offsets can differ between PDF editions. Book settings let you adjust the result if your file includes extra cover pages.

03

Organise your music

Build playlists for rehearsals and gigs

Keep tunes that belong together in a setlist. Open a tune and tap the add-to-playlist button to choose an existing playlist or create a new one.

  1. Open a tune from your search results.
  2. Tap +, then select or create a playlist.
  3. Open Lists from the home screen to browse, rename, reorder, or remove entries from your setlists.

Pull down on the Playlists screen to refresh. Optional device sync can keep playlists available on your other devices.

Optional iCloud sync

Keep TuneFinder in step across your Apple devices.

On iPhone and iPad, TuneFinder can keep a copy of your library in iCloud Documents. Sign in to the same iCloud account on each device and your playlists, book indexes, and connected PDFs can follow you between them.

☁

Set it up

  1. Make sure iCloud Drive is enabled for TuneFinder in iOS or iPadOS Settings.
  2. Open Settings in TuneFinder and tap Enable iCloud sync.
  3. Repeat on your other Apple devices using the same iCloud account, then tap Sync now if needed.
↻

How it behaves

  • Sync runs at startup and after local library edits when your provider is ready.
  • Pull to refresh on Playlists or Book indexes to request an update.
  • Playlist edits are merged across devices, including tune additions, removals and ordering.
  • Pausing sync keeps both the device and iCloud copies intact.
Good to know: TuneFinder stays local-first and works without iCloud. This synchronisation is only between Apple devices. Android connects to a Google Drive account selected in Settings; libraries will not transfer between iOS and Android.
