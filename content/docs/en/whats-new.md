## Help where you need it {#help}
Tap the question-mark button on Search, Playlists, Books or another screen to open a relevant guide. When several topics apply, choose one from the Help menu. Guides open in your browser, in your app language, with instructions for your device selected automatically. You can change the platform in the guide.

## Purchase and restoration in Settings {#purchase-restore}
Open **Settings → Multi-Book Search** to purchase access or restore an existing purchase with the store account that bought it. Search and Books also have clearer navigation icons.

## Your personal PDF library {#library}
TuneFinder imports PDFs into your own library and accepts actual PDFs shared from other apps on iOS and Android. The searchable book list and dedicated details screen make it easier to manage a growing collection.

## Editable OCR indexes {#ocr}
On-device OCR proposes tune titles and PDF page ranges. Review, correct and save those suggestions before search uses them. Detection can be resumed after interruption and rebuilt after an index is removed while its PDF remains available.

## Search, layouts and synchronization {#search-sync}
Free users can select one indexed book for search and change it without deleting other books. Multi-Book Search enables several indexes at once. Bottom navigation, phone/tablet layouts and localized error references help with larger libraries.

Optional synchronization remains platform-specific: iCloud Documents on iOS and a connected Google Drive account on Android. Original source PDFs are preserved by import; draft OCR work remains local until accepted.

## Availability and limits {#limits}
OCR accuracy depends on the PDF and requires review. For purchase and restoration controls, see [Search access and app language](/docs/settings-purchases/).
