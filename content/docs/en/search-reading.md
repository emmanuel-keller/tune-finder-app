## Choose the free search book {#select}
1. Open **Books** and tap a book with an accepted index.
2. Tap **Use for search** if it is not already **Selected for search**.
3. Return to **Search**.

You can change this selection for free. Other books and indexes remain in the library. Multi-Book Search enables simultaneous search across indexed books; synchronized files alone do not unlock it.

## Search a title {#search}
1. Type part of the title in **Search**.
2. Read the book name and PDF page or range beside each result.
3. Tap the desired result to open its PDF.

Search uses accepted index text, not all PDF text. Save OCR suggestions or manual edits before expecting them in search. Repeated titles can appear on different pages or in different books.

![Results distinguish titles, books and page ranges](/screenshots/en/search-results.png)

## Read and add a tune {#read}
Scroll through the PDF pages. Tap a page to hide or reveal the navigation controls. The viewer’s + button adds the displayed tune to a playlist; see [Playlists](/docs/playlists/).

You can also open a book’s PDF from its details without an index. If the result opens the wrong page, [edit the index’s PDF page range](/docs/manual-index/#pages), rather than changing the original PDF.

## Missing results or PDF {#missing}
Check the selected book, saved index, title spelling, and whether the PDF is present. Try a shorter title. An index can be available even when its PDF is missing, so finding a result does not establish that its pages can be opened. Restore the matching PDF in book details and retry.

A playlist reference may retain an older title/page after an index correction. Verify it and replace that playlist entry if needed. [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) covers persistent failures.
